## Fonts

`frontend/src/fonts/` contains Latin subsets of Public Sans, Outfit and JetBrains Mono, each under the SIL Open Font License 1.1. The full licence text sits next to each font (`OFL-*.txt`).

## Knowledge tree data

`backend/data/knowledge/` holds a snapshot of Wikipedia's Vital Articles (level 5), available under CC BY-SA 4.0, and labels from Wikidata, released under CC0. Details are in `backend/data/knowledge/README.md`.
